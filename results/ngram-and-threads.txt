READING
-------
N-gram self-speculation: memory is byte-identical to native (4173 MiB)
in every variant, confirming zero overhead -- no drafter weights, no
drafter KV, and need_n_rs_seq() == 0 so no recurrent-state blowup.
Throughput is also ~identical (18.5-18.9 vs 18.6 native), i.e. ~1.00x.
That is the expected result for these workloads: n-gram speculation pays
on input-grounded generation (summarize, edit, rewrite) where the output
copies from the prompt, and our code/prose suites are open-ended
generation. Acceptance figures here are noisy because draft_n is tiny
and sporadic when few n-gram hits occur.

So n-gram is the right 8 GB fallback -- it costs nothing and cannot hurt
-- but it is not a speedup on open-ended work, and it does not replace a
drafter.

CPU threads: the default (nproc = 14) is the worst setting on this box.
Leaving 2 cores for the OS is worth 1.73x on ternary and 2.27x on 1-bit.
Single 48-token requests, so noisier than the full bench suite, but the
trend is unambiguous and matches the research prediction.
